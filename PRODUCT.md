# Rewind: product one-pager

**What it is:** Browse every site's design history as replayable snapshots

**Who it is for:** The operator of a many-site fleet who redesigns constantly and loses
the previous look the moment it ships.

**Register:** product (a tool someone uses), not brand (a page someone visits).

**The scene:** Late at night mid-redesign, wondering whether the old header actually
looked better, with no way left to check.

**What done looks like:** The old version is on screen, clickable, next to today's, and
"what did it look like before" is answered in under a minute.
