What this is, and what it is made of.

A borrowed manga chart, applied to engineering work, to answer one question: what should you expect from someone working outside the corner they came from? Everything below is the source material, the reading key, and the rules of use.

About this chart

One person's argument about how career effort converts, borrowed from a manga power system. Nothing here is measured, validated, or predictive, and the percentages are illustrative. Use it on yourself. Do not use it to type a colleague, screen a candidate, staff a project, or decide who gets promoted, and do not use it to talk yourself out of a switch you want. Runs entirely in your browser and stores nothing. Hunter x Hunter and Nen are the work of Yoshihiro Togashi, published by Shueisha; this site is unaffiliated commentary and reproduces no art or text from the series.
