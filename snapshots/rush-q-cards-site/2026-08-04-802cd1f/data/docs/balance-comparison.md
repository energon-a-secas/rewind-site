## Priority Legend

- 🔴 **High Priority**: Major balance issues, significantly over/under-valued
- 🟡 **Medium Priority**: Moderate balance issues, needs adjustment
- 🔄 **Minor Priority**: Small adjustments for consistency
- ✅ **Good**: Already balanced or minor tweaks needed
