# Quick Mode Design -- Agent Review Compilation

**Date:** 2026-03-08
**Reviewers:** Tech Lead, Product Manager, Casual Gamer (16yo), Game Designer, Engineering Manager

---

## Unanimous Consensus (5/5 agree)

### Restore Crisis Cards (Section 9e)
Every single reviewer demanded this back. The "oh no" moment when a layoff/crisis card flips is the game's equivalent of UNO's Draw 4 or Exploding Kittens' explode. Without it, project reveals are predictable. Limit to 2-3 cards in the project deck.

### Restore Push Action (Section 9d, reimagined)
"Discard 1 hand card = count as +1 person for commitment this quarter." Gives trailing players a comeback path, creates hand-management tension every turn, and teaches the real tradeoff of burning reserves to meet deadlines. 4/5 explicitly requested this; the 5th (casual gamer) implicitly wanted it via "completing a project should FEEL like something."

### One Action Per Turn is Correct
Every reviewer praised this. Keeps analysis paralysis near zero, rounds moving fast.

### Budget Card Expiry is Strong
The "use it or lose it" rule forces real decisions and creates the social hook for trading. Needs a loud visual cue on the card so new players don't forget.

---

## Strong Consensus (3-4/5 agree)

### Underbidding is Confusing or Weak (4/5)
- Casual gamer: "would cause a 10-minute argument"
- Game designer: "too expensive -- 3-resource swing, near-dead in practice"
- Product manager: "most confusing of the 5 interaction types"
- Tech lead: acknowledged the tension but flagged timing issues

**Recommendation:** Cut underbidding from the base rules. Put it on a reference card as an "advanced variant."

### Q2 Needs a Drama Moment (3/5)
- Tech lead: "Q2 is a dead zone -- add a crisis card flip"
- Product manager: "Q2 is the sweet spot" (disagreed)
- Engineering manager: "bad opening hand makes someone feel out by Q2"

**Recommendation:** Flip 1 crisis/event card at start of Q2. Three-act structure: optimistic hiring (Q1), disruption (Q2), desperate sprint (Q3).

### Tech Lead / Specialist are Undercosted (3/5)
- Tech lead: "strictly better than two separate hires"
- Game designer: "only 1 triangle premium for saving an entire Hire action -- autopick"
- Casual gamer: "'counts as 2' is mentioned once and feels like a cheat"

**Recommendation:** Premium people cost 4 triangles (not 3). Only count as 2 for project commitment, not for agenda purposes.

### Draw Action is Weak (3/5)
- Game designer: "draw 1 is almost never correct when you get 2 free at quarter end"
- Tech lead: implied via hoarding exploit concern
- Engineering manager: implied via comeback mechanics

**Recommendation:** Change Draw to "draw 2, keep 1" (discard the other). Makes it a real choice.

### Drop "CapEx/OpEx" Terminology (2/5 explicit, all implied)
- Casual gamer: "I don't know what capital expenditure means. Just call them triangles and squares."
- Product manager: "two shapes is learnable"

**Recommendation:** In Quick Mode, use ONLY shape names. Reserve CapEx/OpEx for full game.

## Notable Individual Suggestions

### Product Manager: Snake Draft Opening
"Reveal 6 people face-up. Players snake-draft 2 each before Q1." Fixes dead Q1 start, introduces interaction before turn one, teaches what people cards do.

### Tech Lead: Anti-Hoarding Rule
"If you take Draw 2 turns in a row, draw 0 the second time." Prevents passive play.

### Game Designer: Fix Lending Payoff
"Lender gets 2 cards (not 1), or gets +1 rep." Current payoff is too low -- lending will never happen otherwise.

### Engineering Manager: Built-in Retro Phase
"After scoring, each player reveals agenda and answers: 'What did you sacrifice to pursue it?'" Bakes debrief into the game rules, not facilitator-dependent.

### Casual Gamer: Theme is Wallpaper
"I do not care about corporate tech management. Make the mechanics fun enough and the theme becomes wallpaper." This is correct for the casual audience.

### Game Designer: 2-Player Needs a Fix
"Negotiation and lending collapse entirely at 2 players. Need a dummy rival or AI deck that claims uncompleted projects."

## Agenda Design Feedback

- Need 15+ agendas minimum (game designer: "card-counting becomes trivial with 10")
- Agendas must vary in playstyle, not just "collect N of X" (product manager)
- Passive agendas ("Hoarder") vs active agendas ("Networker") have huge difficulty variance -- normalize (game designer)
- Agenda reveal at game end is "gold for debrief" (engineering manager)

---

## Revised Quick Mode Changes Summary

Based on all 5 reviews, the design should be updated with:

1. **Restore crisis cards** (2-3 in project deck)
2. **Restore Push action** (discard 1 card = +1 virtual person)
3. **Cut underbidding** from base rules (advanced variant only)
4. **Add Q2 crisis flip** (1 event card hits all players)
5. **Snake draft opening** (reveal 6 people, draft 2 each)
6. **Buff Draw action** to "draw 2, keep 1"
7. **Buff lending payoff** to 2 cards or +1 rep
8. **Increase premium hire cost** to 4 triangles
9. **Drop CapEx/OpEx language** -- triangles and squares only
10. **Add built-in retro phase** after scoring (agenda reveal + question)
11. **15+ agenda cards** with normalized difficulty
12. **Budget cards need visual expiry indicator**
13. **2-player variant** needs a rival/AI mechanism
