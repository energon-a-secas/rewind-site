## Overview

You're a Tech Manager just hired at Energon Company. You have eight quarters (two fiscal years) to prove yourself the top performer among your peers. Recruit talent, deliver projects, and adapt to sudden events—all while avoiding layoffs and outmaneuvering rival managers in this fast-paced card game.

<br>

## Objective

Accumulate the most **Reputation Points** by:
- Completing projects on time  
- Recruiting, developing and protecting your team  
- Applying Hard, Soft and Power skills effectively  
- Spending favors to negotiate advantages or avert setbacks  

But beware: missed deadlines or unhandled events will cost you reputation and might get you fired.

<br>

## Players & Duration

- **3–5 players**  
- **Duration:** 30–45 minutes  

<br>

## Components

**243 cards**, plus tokensfor progress and dice for quarter tracking:

- **Starter (×53)**: Projects, People, Favor, Bonus Favor, TL;DR
- **Main/Skills (×94)**: Soft Skill, Hard Skill, Power Skill, Bonus Project
- **Business (×65)**: Project Queue, For Hire, Rush Quarter, Layoffs
- **Events (×31)**: Team events and challenges

## Gameplay Flow

The game follows a structured flow across 8 quarters (2 fiscal years). Each quarter consists of two main phases:

### Quarter Preparations and Actions (Round of Turns)
During this phase, each player takes their turn in clockwise order.

**Choosing the First Player:** The most experienced player goes first. If all players are equally experienced, decide by rolling dice, drawing cards, or any other fair method.

**First Turn Setup (Different from Regular Turns):**

1. **Starter Project and Talent Selection**: 
   - Shuffle the starter Project and People cards separately
   - Reveal three face-up project cards
   - Starting with the first player, each player chooses one project card in order
   - Repeat the process with three face-up people cards
   - Place chosen cards in your play area
   - Assign time dice to projects based on their quarter requirements
2. **Initial Draw Phase**: Each player draws 5 cards from the Skills deck to form their starting hand
3. **Play Phase**: Play cards, assign people to projects, use skills or favors
4. **Draw Phase**: Draw 1 card from the Skills deck to end your turn

**Regular Turns (Q2 onwards):**

1. **Event Phase** (Q2+): Draw and resolve one Event card
2. **Play Phase**: Play cards, assign people, use skills or favors
3. **Draw Phase**: Draw 1 card from the Skills deck to end your turn

**Available Resources During Turns:**
- **Project Queue** (Q1+): Three face-up project cards available for purchase
- **For Hire** (Q1+): Three face-up talent cards available for recruitment
- **Skills Deck**: Main source of cards for your hand

### Fast Forward (Quarter Resolution)
After all players complete their turns:

1. **Progress Assignment**: Add progress tokens to active projects (1 per assigned person)
2. **Time Advancement**: Remove time tokens from projects
3. **Rush Quarter** (Q2+): Draw and resolve one Rush Quarter card
4. **Layoffs** (Y2-Q1+): Draw and resolve one Layoff card

### Game Completion
The game ends after 8 quarters (2 fiscal years). The player with the highest reputation wins!

## Play Field Layout

- **Business decks** (left to right):  
  1. Layoffs *(available from Y2-Q1)*
  2. Rush Quarter *(available from Q2)*
  3. Events *(available from Q2)*
  4. Main/Skills Deck *(available from Q1)*
  5. Project Queue *(available from Q1-LP, reveal three face-up cards; the rest remain face-down)*  
  6. For Hire *(available from Q1-LP, reveal three face-up cards; the rest remain face-down)*  
  7. Bonus Favor
  8. Bonus Project

- **Player area**:  
  - Unassigned People (top left): team members waiting for assignment  
  - Active Projects (center): your current projects (start with one)  
  - Bonus Favors (top right, left of starter favors): extra favor cards earned during play  
  - Starter Favors (top right): "It's my first day" card plus any lettered favors  
  - Completed Projects (bottom right): scored and finished projects  
  - Break Tokens: track here; each unauthorized move adds one token, and at three tokens the person quits (−5 Reputation)

## Turn Structure

A quarter is one full cycle in which each player takes a turn, proceeding clockwise. When everyone has acted, the quarter ends with company-wide resolutions (Rush Quarter and Layoff) before the next quarter begins. There are eight quarters in total, representing two fiscal years of hiring, project delivery and crisis management.

### Phases

| Phase            | When It Occurs                     |
|------------------|------------------------------------|
| Turn             | Each player's individual turn      |
| Progress         | After all turns, advance projects  |
| Rush Quarter     | From quarter 2 onwards, end of quarter |
| Layoff           | From Y2-Q1 onwards, end of quarter |

### First Turn

Your first turn introduces the core workflow:

1. **Draw 1** starter Project and place it in front of you.  
   - Add time tokens equal to its required quarters.  
2. **Draw 1** starter Person and place them in your Unassigned area.  
3. **Draw 5** cards from the Main/Skills Deck.  
4. **Play** cards and **assign** people:  
   - Announce each card's effect, resolve it, then discard the card.  
   - Opponents may nullify the effect if the card allows.  
   - To assign a person, move them from Unassigned to the project area.  
5. **End of turn:** draw **2** cards from the Main/Skills Deck.  

> **End of Q1 unlock**  
> - Reveal three cards from Project Queue face-up.  
> - Each player may buy one per turn; after purchase, immediately draw and replace to keep three cards available.  

> **After all players act**  
> - **Progress Phase**  
>   - If nothing prevents progress, add one progress token per person on each active project.  
>   - Remove one time token from each project.  
> - **Rush Quarter** (from quarter 2): draw and resolve one card.  
> - **Layoff** (from Y2-Q1): draw and resolve one card.  

### Regular Turns (from quarter 2)

1. **Event Phase**: draw and resolve one Event card.  
2. **Play Phase**: play cards, assign or reassign people, use skills or favors.  
3. **End Phase**: draw one card from the Main/Skills Deck.  

> **End of Q2 unlock**  
> - Reveal three cards from For Hire face-up.  
> - Each player may buy one per turn; after purchase, immediately draw and replace to keep three cards available.  

> **After all players act**  
> - **Progress Phase**: advance and resolve projects as above.  
> - **Rush Quarter**: draw and resolve one card.  
> - **Layoff** (from Y2-Q1): draw and resolve one card.

## Interacting with Players

Use favors to trade resources or request assistance. Well-timed negotiations can avert crises, save people from layoffs or earn extra cards.

<br>

## Strategy Tips

- Balance progress with hand size—aim to hold at least three to four cards.  
- Time your purchases to grab key projects or hires as soon as decks unlock.  
- Spend favors proactively rather than waiting for emergencies.  
- Stay flexible and adapt your plan as projects finish and events unfold.

## Timing & Triggers

Cards have specific timing for when their effects activate:

| Trigger/Timing     | Description                                    |
|--------------------|------------------------------------------------|
| When Completed     | Effect activates when a project is completed   |
| Passive Effect     | Effect is always active                        |
| Continuous Effect  | Effect persists for multiple turns             |
| Effect             | Effect activates immediately when played       |
| Permanent          | Effect cannot be removed once applied          |
| Conditional        | Effect activates under specific conditions     |
| Multi-Target       | Effect can target multiple players/projects    |

**Note:** Some cards have multiple timing options separated by commas or semicolons, allowing players to choose when to activate effects.

## Advanced Game Modes

For experienced players looking for new challenges, Rush Q! offers several advanced game modes:

### History Mode
Play through one of three high-pressure corporate scandal scenarios inspired by real-world events:

**• "Blood Test Revolution" Goes Bust** - A biotech startup scandal where fraudulent technology claims create massive pressure to deliver impossible results. Teams must navigate regulatory scrutiny while maintaining impossible deadlines.

**• "Numbers Don't Lie" - Until They Do** - Corporate accounting fraud creates a culture of deception and unrealistic expectations. Players face constant pressure to meet inflated targets while avoiding detection.

**• "WeWork: From Unicorn to Unraveling"** - A unicorn startup's rapid expansion and questionable business model leads to intense scrutiny and impossible growth demands. Teams must deliver results while the company burns through cash.

**History Mode Rules:**
- Start with History Event card in playfield
- Reduced hand size (3 cards max)
- All projects require +1 quarter
- Layoffs trigger every quarter from Q1
- Break tokens accumulate 50% faster

### Single Player Mode
Solo challenge with predefined projects and talent pool:

**Easy Mode:**
- 4 card hand size
- 5 projects to complete
- Standard project requirements
- AI draws 1 Event per quarter

**Medium Mode:**
- 3 card hand size  
- 7 projects to complete
- +1 quarter on all projects
- AI draws 2 Events per quarter

**Hard Mode:**
- 2 card hand size
- 9 projects to complete
- +2 quarters on all projects
- AI draws 3 Events per quarter
- No favor cards available

### Cooperative Missions
Team-based survival mode where all players must survive to win:

**Cooperative Rules:**
- All players must achieve positive reputation to win
- Favor cards can be shared between players
- Team Mission cards take precedence over individual goals
- If any player reaches negative reputation, all players lose
- Team Mission completions benefit all players equally

### Rush Q! Hardcore Mode
Extreme pressure mode for the most experienced players:

**Hardcore Rules:**
- Only 4 quarters instead of 8
- All projects need twice the normal progress
- Hand size reduced by 1 card
- Events trigger every player turn
- Layoff cards available from Q2
- Favor trading completely disabled
- Break tokens accumulate twice as fast
- Reputation points are halved for all actions
