Interactions

Two Truths and a Lie

Each person shares two truths and one lie. Teams guess which one is the lie. Simple way to learn more about each other.

Show and Tell

Bring something from home (a hobby, pet, or memorable item) and share its story. Builds connections without any tool.

Activities

Draw a geometrical figure from instructions

One person describes a shape step by step; others draw. Shows how we dimension things differently and how details get missed or misunderstood. No link needed.

Everyone shares up to 5 pictures of something that brought them joy. Great conversation starter.

Describe the last sprint with a song

Before the retro, ask everyone to pick a song that sums up how the last sprint felt. During the session, each person shares the title and a short reason. It’s a light way to surface moods and themes before the main discussion.

Suggested songs (already used)

  • Highway to Hell — AC/DC
  • Under Pressure — Queen & David Bowie
  • Eye of the Tiger — Survivor
  • Don’t Stop Believin’ — Journey
  • Fix You — Coldplay
  • Shake It Off — Taylor Swift
